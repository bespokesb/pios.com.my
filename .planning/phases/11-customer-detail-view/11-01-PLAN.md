<objective>
Build CRM-03: a Filament `ViewCustomer` detail page (ViewRecord) in the `/business` panel that shows a customer's core info via a `CustomerInfolist`, plus two Relation Managers (`NotesRelationManager`, `FollowUpsRelationManager`) listing/creating/editing/deleting the customer's notes and follow-ups — all routed through the resource's already-scoped `getEloquentQuery()` so no Business Owner can open another BO's customer or its child records.

Purpose: The `/business` panel currently has only List/Create/Edit customer pages — no detail view. This is the anchor CRM screen that renders the Phase 10 notes/follow-ups. It MUST be a `ViewRecord` (inherits `visibleTo` scope for free) and the child Relation Managers MUST stamp `business_owner_id` server-side, never trust a form field — otherwise this is the exact surface where the BO-ownership invariant leaks (PITFALLS.md #1, #2, #10).
Output: `ViewCustomer` page + `CustomerInfolist` + two Relation Managers (owner-stamped + audited + scoped), wired into `CustomerResource` (getPages 'view', infolist(), getRelations()), a `ViewAction` on the customers table, bilingual labels, and a Feature test proving a BO views their own customer + child records while another BO is denied. Full suite green.

Depends on Phase 10 (10-crm-data-layer): consumes `App\Models\CustomerNote`, `App\Models\CustomerFollowUp` (their `#[Fillable]`, casts, `scopeVisibleTo`, `customer()`), `Customer::notes()/followUps()` HasMany, and the registered child policies. Phase 10 runs first (sequential by phase); this plan does NOT recreate them.

Out of scope (explicit): NO changes to the customer create/edit form (`CustomerForm`, `CreateCustomer`, `EditCustomer`). NO WhatsApp/Call actions (those are Phase 13 / CRM-06). NO list segments/filters/tabs (Phase 12 / CRM-04). NO new migration or model. The Phone helper is NOT wired here.
</objective>
