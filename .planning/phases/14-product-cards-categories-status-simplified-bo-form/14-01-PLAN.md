<objective>
Turn the BO-facing `/business` product screen into a card/grid listing with category + status filters (PLST-01, PLST-02) and simplify the BO product create/edit form to its six essentials, hard-gating the admin-only fields cost/vendor/sales_commission/classification at both the schema level and server-side save (PLST-03).

Purpose: A Business Owner manages their own catalog visually (image/price/status cards) and through a guided form that never exposes or persists commercial-confidential fields. `classification` (Amway/Non-Amway) drives Rule 2 eligibility, so a BO must NEVER be able to set it — BO products default to Non-Amway server-side.
Output: A panel-aware `ProductsTable` (business → contentGrid cards; admin/partner → existing tabular columns untouched), category + status `SelectFilter`s on all panels, a field-gated `ProductForm` with a hardened image upload, server-side admin-key stripping in Create/Edit pages, bilingual labels, and tests proving own-only listing, filters, schema-level field exclusion, crafted-save block, and SVG/oversize rejection — with zero regressions.

Out of scope: WhatsApp share/promote (PLST-04 → Phase 15), the CRM data layer / notes / follow-ups (Phases 10–13), and any public product page (deferred to v2 SHARE-PUB-01). Do NOT use `App\Support\Phone` here.
</objective>
